Featured Project

Disaster Management System

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript

The Disaster Management System is a clean and accessible web portal built to assist people during emergencies such as floods, cyclones, and earthquakes. The goal of this project is to centralize vital emergency information in one reliable place so that citizens and responders can act quickly.

Key Features:

  • Emergency Helplines: Quick access to verified contact numbers for disaster response teams, ambulance services, fire, and police.
  • Incident Reporting: A simple form allowing victims or volunteers to submit urgent reports regarding affected locations and immediate needs.
  • Shelter Directory: Organized list of safe emergency shelters and relief camps.
  • Preparedness Guidelines: Easy-to-read safety instructions and dos-and-don'ts for emergency situations.
